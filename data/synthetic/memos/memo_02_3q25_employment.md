# Note: 3Q 2025 employment growth

**To:** Senior researcher  **Date:** 10 November 2025

Total employment grew by **24,800** in 3Q 2025, up from 10,400 in 2Q 2025 and above the 22,300 recorded in 3Q 2024. Growth came from both residents and non-residents, with non-resident gains concentrated in Construction and Manufacturing.

Retrenchments were 3,500 in 3Q 2025 (1.4 per 1,000 employees), comparable to 3,540 in the previous quarter. Citizen unemployment in September 2025 was 3.0%.

*Source: MOM Labour Market Advance Release, 3Q 2025 (30 October 2025).*
