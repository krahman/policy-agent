# Note: 2Q 2024 retrenchments

**To:** Labour market desk  **Date:** 12 August 2024

Following the Labour Market Advance Release of 31 July 2024, retrenchments held steady in 2Q 2024 at **3,100**, compared with 3,030 in 1Q 2024. Business reorganisation or restructuring remained the top reason cited.

On this basis the desk should describe retrenchments as flat quarter on quarter in the next brief. Overall unemployment declined to 2.0% in June 2024 (resident 2.7%, citizen 2.8%).

*Source for all figures: MOM Labour Market Advance Release, 2Q 2024. Full quarterly report not yet published at the time of writing.*
